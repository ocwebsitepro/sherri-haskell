For Financial Professionals

Your clients are making Medicare decisions.
Are those decisions aligned with their financial plan?

For high-income clients, Medicare isn’t just a healthcare decision — it’s a financial event. IRMAA surcharges, income timing around RMDs and Roth conversions, capital gains events — the intersections are numerous, consequential, and frequently overlooked.

1. Overlooking IRMAA Planning

The two-year lookback creates delayed premium impacts. Income events can trigger significant and unexpected surcharges that catch clients off guard.

2. Treating Medicare as a Commodity

Plan selection based on premium alone consistently leads to higher long-term costs and coverage gaps that affect quality of care.

3. Lack of Financial Integration

Decisions made outside the planning framework miss opportunities to optimize income structure, reduce tax exposure, and protect assets.
